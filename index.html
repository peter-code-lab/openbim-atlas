Where mandates meet business value

Mandate trackers list where openBIM is required. This page connects that policy map to documented project outcomes — so the headline isn't a list of countries, it's a relationship: where governments require open standards, measurable value follows.

1 · Policy

Where is openBIM required?

Two different kinds of mandate, often confused: governments requiring openBIM as a client (public procurement) and requiring it as a regulator (building-permit submission & approval). The map shows either one.

2 · Practice

What does openBIM deliver?

Documented project results — cost, time and carbon savings from real projects, mostly buildingSMART Awards case studies, each linked to its source. Shown as blue markers on the map.

3 · The link

Do mandates and value go together?

The correlation this project exists to show: documented value clusters in jurisdictions that mandate open standards. The panel below counts the evidence.

Every claim on this page carries its source — click any country for its mandate details, effective dates and source links. Compiled July 2026 from official programme documents, legislation and buildingSMART publications (full list in the footer).

The correlation, counted

Two distributions, same four rows. Gray: how the tracked jurisdictions spread across mandate stages. Blue: where the documented value cases actually happened. If policy and value were unrelated, blue would roughly mirror gray. Instead, every documented case sits in the top two rows — where openBIM is required or a mandate is phasing in — and none where it's voluntary or absent.
